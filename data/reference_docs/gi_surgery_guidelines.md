# Synthetic GI Surgery Coding Guidelines

Exploratory laparotomy is a broad operative entry procedure. In this demo reference set, CPT 49000 may be considered when the documented service is exploration without a more specific definitive abdominal procedure.

Small bowel resection with primary anastomosis maps to the enterectomy family. CPT 44120 is used in this synthetic schedule when the note documents resection of small intestine with a single anastomosis.

Partial colectomy with anastomosis maps to the colectomy family. CPT 44140 is used in this synthetic schedule when the note documents removal of part of the colon with primary anastomosis.

When laparotomy, bowel resection, and stapled anastomosis are documented together, coder review should verify whether the exploratory laparotomy is separately reportable or included in the definitive bowel resection.

Documentation should identify the bowel segment, extent of resection, anastomosis method, indication, findings, and complications.

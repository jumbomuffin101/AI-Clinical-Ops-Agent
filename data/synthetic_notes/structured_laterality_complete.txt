Title: Structured left inguinal hernia repair

Indication: Synthetic patient with symptomatic left inguinal hernia.

Procedure: Left open inguinal hernia repair with mesh.

Findings: Indirect left inguinal hernia without obstruction.

Technique: A left groin incision was made. The hernia sac was reduced and mesh was secured to the inguinal ligament and conjoint tendon.

Implants: Synthetic mesh.

Complications: None.

Closure: External oblique fascia and skin were closed in layers.

Postoperative diagnosis: Left inguinal hernia.
